#  Global Oil Flow Tracker

## What is this?
An interactive world map that visualises global oil production, consumption and trade flows in real time. Built to understand how oil moves around the world and which countries are the biggest players.

---

## What you can explore
- **Top oil producers** — which countries pump the most oil and where it goes
- **Top oil consumers** — which economies depend most on oil imports
- **Trade flows** — major shipping routes between producers and consumers
- **Price by region** — how regional supply/demand affects pricing
- **Geopolitical risk overlay** — how conflicts and sanctions redirect oil flows

---

---

## Why this matters
Oil doesn't just flow freely it's shaped by sanctions, pipelines, tanker routes, political alliances and geography. This dashboard makes those invisible flows visible.
